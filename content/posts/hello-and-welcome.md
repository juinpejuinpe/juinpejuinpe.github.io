Every website needs a first post, and this is mine. It's a small announcement,
really: this corner of the internet now exists, and I intend to keep filling
it with words.

## What you'll find here

Essays about the craft of writing. Stories that started as half-formed
sentences on the backs of receipts. Notes on the books I'm reading, the walks
I take, and the questions I keep turning over.

It won't be a perfectly curated feed. It will be honest, and it will be mine.

## Why write publicly?

There is something particular about writing for an audience, even a small one.
The sentences get clearer. The half-thoughts either harden into arguments or
quietly dissolve. A public page is a kind of accountability: it asks me to
finish things.

It's also a gift to my future self. Five years from now, I want to be able to
look back at who I was — not through a photo album, but through the ideas I
was brave enough to put down in ink.

## A note on the tea

The tea is doing very well, thank you. It's a jasmine green, and it has
achieved the perfect temperature for writing.

That's the state of things. The desk is set, the page is blank, and the words
are starting to arrive. I hope you'll stay a while.
